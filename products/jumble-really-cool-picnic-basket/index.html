Really Cool Insulated Picnic Basket

Ready to upgrade your picnic game? This basket is the ultimate way to carry your essentials in style! Perfect for everything from picnics and dinner parties to beach days and more!

This basket is designed to keep your food and drinks fresh with its fully insulated, heat-sealed, leak-proof lining. Whether you're packing cold drinks for a picnic or keeping snacks chilled on the go, it’s got you covered.

The waterproof inner lining makes wiping up messes a breeze, while the durable polyester outer ensures it stays looking great. Just keep the zip lid upright when in use, as it’s not waterproof.

With extra-wide velcro attachments and a strong aluminium frame, this basket can handle up to 15kg of picnic goodies. And with the matching print, it’s as stylish as it is practical! When you're done, simply remove the support struts and fold it down flat for easy storage. It’s lightweight and comes with a handy carry bag for when you need to stash it away.

The zip lid is fully removable, and with comfy padded handles, you can carry this basket any way you like - whether it’s a casual trip to the beach or a more formal dinner party.

No more rummaging for your keys or phone! This basket comes with a zip pocket to keep your valuables safe and separate from the rest of your things. Plus, it has four little plastic feet to keep your basket elevated and clean.

A NOTE ABOUT COLOURS

It's our mission to make sure our images accurately represent our products.  We take photos of our products outside in full sunlight, outside on a cloudy day, and inside in a studio with studio lighting to reflect how the colours of the product appear under various lighting conditions. While we have made every attempt to show textures, appearances and colours as faithfully as possible, what you see can vary depending on your monitor, phone screen or computer equipment. Please view our images on a range of different devices before making a purchase.

Colours may vary slightly between different products of the same design, and between different production runs of the same products.

The artwork on our products is a high-quality photographic reproduction of a real painting on canvas - so yes, that gorgeous texture and those little brushstroke details? Totally part of the charm!

Because our products come in all shapes and sizes, the artwork will look a bit different depending on what you're buying. Smaller goodies like tote bags and picnic baskets show a more “zoomed out” version of the artwork - so the details are mini and cute and more importantly, suit the size of the product. Bigger items like our picnic rugs and wet things bags (made from offcuts of our really big picnic rugs!) give you a close-up view, with bigger, bolder details that really pop.
